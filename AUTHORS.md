# Authors and attribution

Yao Xu ([@cadamcat](https://github.com/cadamcat)) is the author and maintainer of this formalization project.

The existence of irrationality sequences is known: Erdős gave the example `2^(2^n)`, and Kovač and Tao proved existence with any prescribed super-exponential growth. This repository does not claim a new mathematical result; its proof uses a direct construction and an elementary tail estimate.

The author planned, directed and reviewed the work, using AI coding assistants; all proofs and their dependencies are checked by the Lean kernel.

The project uses Mathlib. Its authors and license, and the Formal Conjectures definition this project adapts, are credited in [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).
